Gym Rules (scroll box)

1. Re-rack your weights after use.

2. Wipe down equipment after every set.

3. Wear clean training shoes at all times.

4. Respect other members and share equipment.

5. Ask a coach if you are unsure how to use a machine.

6. No dropping dumbbells outside the lifting platforms.

7. Keep your phone volume off on the training floor.
